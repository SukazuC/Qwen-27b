# Claims Review

## Ingredient Benefits

All ingredient benefit claims require legal/regulatory review before launch.
Claims must comply with EU Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006 on nutrition and health claims.

| Ingredient | Claim | Status | Notes |
|-----------|-------|--------|-------|

| Magnésium | "Contribue au fonctionnement musculaire normal" | Needs validation | Standard EFSA-approved claim |
| Zinc | "Contribue à protéger les cellules contre le stress oxydatif" | Needs validation | Standard EFSA-approved claim |

## Marketing Claims

| Claim | Status | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| "Zéro sucre" | Needs validation | Must match product nutritional declaration |
| "Vegan" | Needs validation | Must be substantiated with certification or supply chain review |
| "Fabriqué en France" | Needs validation | Must meet EU origin labeling requirements |
| "prévenir les crampes" | **BLOCKED** | Do NOT use unless legal/regulatory approval confirms |
